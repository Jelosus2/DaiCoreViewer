# DaiCoreViewer

An unofficial, browser-based viewer for **Daiblos Core** character animations, skins, archives, and ASMR.

Explore the game's artwork and interactions through a responsive interface with audio playback, captions and English translations*.

[Report a bug](https://github.com/Jelosus2/DaiCoreViewer/issues) · [Ko-fi](https://ko-fi.com/jelosus1) · [Patreon](https://www.patreon.com/cw/jelosus1)

## Features

- Browse individual characters and skins with artwork previews and search.
- Explore ASMR, Archives, Ungrouped, and Other assets.
- Play character interactions and minigames.
- Listen to interaction audio with synchronized captions.
- Control ASMR playback with play, pause, replay, and seeking.
- Adjust volume and toggle captions or interaction markers.
- Zoom, pan and fullscreen.
- Switch game text between original Chinese and unofficial AI-translated English.
- Choose how often entrance animations play.

## Using the viewer

1. Choose a category and select an asset.
2. Click or tap the artwork to trigger supported interactions.
3. Enable interaction markers to help locate active hotspots.
4. Use the canvas controls to adjust audio, captions, zoom, panning, or fullscreen.
5. Open **Settings** to change the game-text language and entrance-animation preference.

For assets with an ASMR interaction, activating its hotspot opens the corresponding ASMR entry.

\* English translations are unofficial AI translations and may contain inaccuracies or adapted wording. Text without a matching translation falls back to the original Chinese. The language setting applies to supported game text; the viewer interface is in English.

## Reporting bugs

Please [open an issue](https://github.com/Jelosus2/DaiCoreViewer/issues) and include:

- Your browser, operating system, and device.
- The asset name, skin, and ID when available.
- Steps to reproduce the problem.
- Expected and actual behavior.
- A screenshot, recording, or console error if useful.

For audio or caption problems, also mention the interaction and selected text language.
